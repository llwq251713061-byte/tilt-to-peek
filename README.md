# Tilt to Peek

Tilt your phone to look around inside a picture.

This is **not real 3D**. Each picture is a single image turned into a 360° orbit video ([MiniMax H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3) + the [ORB360 LoRA](https://huggingface.co/MATLOWAI/MiniMax-H3-ORB360-CardSpin) by MATLOW). The page splits the video into frames and shows the frame that matches your phone's tilt.

Pictures and manga were made with AI.
