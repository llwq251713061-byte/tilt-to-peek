Tilt to Peek

Tilt to Peek tilt your phone to look around

Settings

Tilt your phone left and right to look around (the way you hold it when it starts is the front; Recenter resets it). On a computer, move the pointer left and right, or drag a panel to turn just that one. This is not real 3D: the page picks a pre-rendered video frame to match your tilt. Original demo videos use MiniMax H3 + the ORB360 LoRA by MATLOW. The Blue Maid scene uses a user-provided Jimeng-generated video.
